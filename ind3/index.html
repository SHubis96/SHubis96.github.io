DIRECTIONS

We are located in the Northwest of the Streets of Tarkov, just a short walk from the main square.

  1. Head north from the main square.
  2. Turn left at the first intersection.
  3. Continue straight for about 200 meters.
  4. You will find Tarkov Tavern on your left-hand side.
