Turn Table is a family-owned shop that has been selling vinyl records for decades. We carry new releases, used classics, and rare finds. Stop by our store to explore our collection, and listen to something new on our in-store turntables!

Our staff is passionate about music and dedicated to helping you find the perfect record. We are always happy to share recommendations and provide information about our collection.

We also buy and trade vinyl records, helping you expand your collection or find that elusive album you've been searching for.
